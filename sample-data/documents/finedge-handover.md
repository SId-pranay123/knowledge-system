# FinEdge Research Assistant – Handover Notes
Date: 2025-03-18
Authors: Rahul Mehta, Arjun Reddy

## What was delivered
- RAG system over ~400 internal research reports and market notes
- Simple web UI for analysts
- Basic feedback mechanism (thumbs up/down)

## What worked
- Good enough for “find me reports about X”
- Fast to build

## What did not work well
- No understanding of how reports relate to each other (series, updates, contradictions)
- Could not answer “what changed in our view on topic Y since last quarter”
- Analysts still kept personal notes outside the system

## Lesson
Pure document retrieval is useful but limited. 
For knowledge work, relationships and evolution of ideas matter a lot.
This lesson influenced our later thinking on Lexora and the internal KB.
