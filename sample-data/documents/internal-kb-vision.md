# Internal Knowledge Base – Vision (Draft)
Author: Sneha Patel
Date: 2025-07-18

## Problem
We are 12 people. Already we lose context:
- Why did we choose approach X for FinEdge?
- Who worked on the last healthcare exploration?
- What decisions did we already make about model vendors?

Information lives in Notion pages, Slack threads, Google Docs and people’s memories.

## Desired outcomes
1. New joiners can understand past projects and decisions faster
2. We stop re-asking the same questions
3. We can see connections between clients, projects, technical choices and lessons learned

## Principles
- Structure matters more than fancy search in the beginning
- Capture decisions with context (who, when, why)
- Keep the barrier to adding information low
- Prefer clear relationships over complex AI features in v1

## Out of scope for v1
- Full Slack history import
- Automatic meeting transcription
- Complex permission systems
