# MediSync – Discovery Summary
Date: 2025-11-28
Author: Vikram Singh

## Client context
MediSync Health wants clinicians to find relevant protocols and guidelines faster. Current pain:
- Guidelines stored in different systems
- Hard to know which internal protocol overrides or extends an external guideline
- Research papers are saved but rarely linked back to practice

## Key insight from first workshops
Doctors think in terms of “this condition → these guidelines → our hospital’s adaptation → recent papers”. 
The connections are more important than any single document.

## Risk
We do not yet have strong clinical domain expertise on the team. 
Decision: keep project in discovery until we have a clinical advisor (see decision d004).

## Possible direction
A knowledge layer that explicitly models:
- Condition
- Guideline
- Internal protocol
- Evidence / paper
and the relationships between them.
